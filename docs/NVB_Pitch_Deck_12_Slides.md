## 2–3 Line Brief Description (for submission form)
Nexus VisBharath (NVB) is a multilingual, voice-first AI platform that converts fragmented citizen feedback into ranked district-level infrastructure priorities. It combines live Google AI (Gemini on Vertex AI and Google STT) with public-sector planning signals to surface demand hotspots and recommend high-impact projects. Built as a Digital Public Good, NVB is designed to scale across states and communities in India.
