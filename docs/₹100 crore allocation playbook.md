# ₹100 Crore Allocation Playbook (NVB Score-Based)

## 1) Budget Split (simple, defensible)
- **₹70 Cr — Core Priority Pool** (top-ranked NVB projects)
- **₹20 Cr — Equity Protection Pool** (high-deprivation districts)
- **₹10 Cr — Rapid Response Pool** (emerging spikes/emergencies)

## 2) NVB Composite Score (0–100)
Use a transparent weighted score:
- **30% Deprivation**
- **30% Infrastructure Gap**
- **25% Demand Velocity/Volume**
- **15% Human Capital ROI**

`NVB Score = 0.30*Dep + 0.30*Gap + 0.25*Demand + 0.15*ROI`

## 3) Project Tiers (decision rule)
- **Tier A (Score ≥ 75):** Immediate funding
- **Tier B (Score 60–74):** Fund if budget remains, or phase-wise
- **Tier C (< 60):** Queue, redesign, or merge into larger project

## 4) ₹100 Cr Allocation Logic

- **Core Priority Pool (₹70 Cr)**
  - 70% to Tier A districts/projects
  - 30% to top Tier B with strong feasibility/readiness

- **Equity Protection Pool (₹20 Cr)**
  - Reserved for districts in top deprivation quartile
  - Minimum ticket: ₹2 Cr per selected district cluster
  - Can fund Tier B if deprivation is very high

- **Rapid Response Pool (₹10 Cr)**
  - Trigger when demand velocity rises >30% month-on-month
  - 72-hour approval window for temporary but targeted interventions

## 5) Guardrails (to avoid misuse)
- **District cap:** max 15% of total budget to any one district (₹15 Cr)
- **Category cap:** max 40% to one category (avoid all-money-to-one-sector bias)
- **Minimum spread:** at least 8 districts receive funding in this cycle
- **Readiness check mandatory:** land/department feasibility before release

## 6) Example Distribution (illustrative)
- Tier A Water + Sanitation clusters across 5 districts: **₹38 Cr**
- Tier A Roads/Transport clusters across 4 districts: **₹21 Cr**
- Tier B Health/Education upgrades (high ROI): **₹11 Cr**
- Equity pool (underserved districts): **₹20 Cr**
- Rapid response (seasonal outbreaks/flooded roads): **₹10 Cr**

Total: **₹100 Cr**

## 7) Monitoring & Reallocation (every 60 days)
- Track:
  - demand reduction %
  - SLA adherence
  - utilization %
- **Reallocate 20% of underperforming funds** to better-performing Tier A/B projects.
- If demand does not decline after completion, trigger technical audit.

## 8) 3 KPI targets for pitch
- **≥25% demand reduction** in funded clusters within 6 months
- **≥90% budget utilization** with milestone-linked releases
- **≥40% spend in high-deprivation geographies** (equity compliance)
