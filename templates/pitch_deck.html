Slide 02 · The Governance Challenge

The Municipal Infrastructure Gap

Why 65% of citizen grievances in India's 4,000+ Urban Local Bodies (ULBs) fail to reach capital expenditure (Capex) budgets.

Multilingual Ingress Friction

Citizens voice complaints in Tamil, Telugu, Hindi, and regional dialects via WhatsApp, IVR, or SMS. Portals lack automated indicative triage, leaving requests trapped in manual queues.

Siloed Isolated Demands

1,000 individual reports of pipe bursts or road damage are treated as 1,000 isolated tickets rather than a single aggregated capital works requirement.

Subjective Capex Allocation

Municipal budget allocations lack objective linkage to real citizen stress indicators, PM Gati Shakti nodes, or NITI Aayog deprivation data.

Audit Opacity

No verifiable cryptographic audit trail exists connecting a citizen's complaint to the eventual Detailed Project Report (DPR) and municipal sanction.

Slide 03 · Solution Architecture

The 5-Layer Federated Intelligence Architecture

An end-to-end Digital Public Good transforming raw regional citizen voice into accountable capital investments.

Layer 1: Multilingual Omnichannel Ingress

Tamil, Telugu, English voice & text via Web, WhatsApp, IVR, SMS. Real-time PII sanitization (Aadhaar/phone redaction).

Layer 2: Gemini 3.6 Flash Indic Triage

Zero-shot category tagging (10 categories), urgency classification, sentiment, and instant emergency priority dispatch.

Layer 3: Spatial Intelligence & Clustering

Request clustering links related reports; geographic filters organize the synthetic demonstration across 97 configured districts.

Layer 4: National Data Fusion (DPI)

Fuses citizen demand with PM Gati Shakti infrastructure nodes, NITI Aayog MPI poverty index, and Census 2011 demographics.

Layer 5: Auditor Workbench & Tamper-Evident Ledger

Role-separated municipal analyst workspace and auditor workbench backed by SHA-256 cryptographic chaining and Merkle verification.

Slide 07 · Layer 5 Accountability

Auditor Workbench & Tamper-Evident Receipts

Tamper-evident records connect proposals to citizen evidence and independent human review.
