What the jury can follow

  1. 01 · ListenVoice, text and messaging carry the citizen’s own language.
  2. 02 · UnderstandTranslate, classify urgency and category, and identify the responsible department.
  3. 03 · AggregateGroup related requests by place and need; distinguish report volume from unmet need.
  4. 04 · PrioritizeCompare demand with population, deprivation, coverage gaps and potential funding.
  5. 05 · DeliverRecord officer actions, execution milestones, deadlines and escalation.
  6. 06 · EvaluateTrack service outcomes and test impact against credible comparison areas.
