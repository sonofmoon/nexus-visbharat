Governance & Evaluator Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Detailed technical clarifications regarding privacy, algorithmic governance, and enterprise deployment.

How does Nexus VisBharat ensure compliance with India's DPDP Act 2023? ▼

Nexus VisBharat applies a zero-trust edge sanitization policy. Citizen submissions undergo deterministic regex and NER PII scrubbing (masking 12-digit Aadhaar numbers, phone numbers, and full names) prior to database insertion and LLM inference. Telemetry logs record zero raw prompt data. All analytical views use aggregated k-anonymity clustering to ensure individual citizen identities cannot be re-identified.

How does the system prevent LLM hallucinations in capital budget allocations? ▼

LLMs are never allowed to compute financial budgets autonomously. Generative AI (Gemini Flash) is restricted solely to unstructured text/speech extraction, translation, and structured summarization. Capital allocation scores (Strategic Prioritization Score, SPS v2.4) are calculated using a 100% deterministic mathematical formula based on verified urgency, demographic deprivation indices, and spatial clustering. Furthermore, final budgetary decisions require human-in-the-loop municipal approval.

Why is the audit ledger designated as "tamper-evident" rather than "immutable"? ▼

In alignment with transparent software engineering ethics, we use the scientifically accurate term tamper-evident. Each log entry stores the cryptographic SHA-256 hash of its predecessor, creating a verifiable blockchain-style cryptographic sequence. Any manual modification or tampering with a historical record immediately breaks the hash chain and triggers automated validation alerts in the auditor console (/auditor).

How does NVB maintain continuity if Google AI APIs experience latency or disconnects? ▼

NVB features a deterministic FastPath Emergency Fallback. If Vertex AI or Google AI REST endpoints timeout or fail, the platform falls back to deterministic rule-based keyword extraction and urgency scoring in <800ms. High-severity hazards (e.g., collapsed bridges, electrocution risks, toxic water contamination) are automatically escalated to municipal emergency channels without stalling the citizen intake pipeline.

What is the transition path from this hackathon demonstration to a live ministry rollout? ▼

The production code contains documented deployment pathways for connecting Cloud SQL PostgreSQL with automated private IP peering and IAM authentication. The platform is pre-architected as an Apache 2.0 Digital Public Good (DPG), ready to interface directly with existing national platforms like CPGRAMS, UMANG, and State Integrated Grievance Redressal Systems.
