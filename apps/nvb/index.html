Governance & Evaluator Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Detailed technical clarifications regarding privacy, algorithmic governance, and enterprise deployment.

What privacy safeguards does NVB implement? ▾

NVB applies pattern-based masking of recognized identifiers on selected intake paths. Private records may retain original submission content; complete anonymization is not guaranteed. Public views withhold citizen text, contact details and exact locations. Differential privacy is optional for public aggregates, with a default epsilon of 0.75 when enabled. These controls do not constitute legal compliance certification.

How are AI-assisted interpretation and funding decisions separated? ▾

AI-assisted classifications feed explicit planning rules. Recorded urgency and reference indicators remain subject to review. Scoring assumptions and cost estimates are inspectable, and project decisions require authorized human approval. Synthetic approvals do not represent government sanctions or expenditure.

Why is the audit ledger designated as "tamper-evident" rather than "immutable"? ▾

Audit events are linked through SHA-256 hashes. Hash-chain verification can detect inconsistencies when run; it does not guarantee immediate detection or protection against an administrator rewriting the chain. Independently retained checkpoints can support comparison. External anchoring remains a proposed design, not a verified deployment.

How does NVB maintain continuity if Google AI APIs experience latency or disconnects? ▾

NVB includes deterministic local hazard screening and manual-review paths when provider processing is unavailable. The recorded 600-request local microbenchmark measured 0.021 ms median internal screening time, excluding HTTP, database, cloud and delivery latency. This is not an end-to-end response-time guarantee. High-severity signals can be routed to configured escalation paths; confirmed responder delivery requires connector and receipt evidence.

What is the transition path from this pilot deployment to a live ministry rollout? ▾

The repository documents a deployment path using external PostgreSQL, named identities, provider configuration and recovery checks. Operational opening also requires participating authorities, reviewed geography, data responsibilities and recorded acceptance evidence. NVB is Apache 2.0 software designed with Digital Public Good principles; it does not claim formal DPGA recognition or endorsement. Potential integration with CPGRAMS, UMANG or state systems requires agency coordination, data-sharing arrangements and tested connectors.
